## Plan tasks

This applies to a task whose result is `plan.md`.

- Fail the round when `plan.md` leaves a worker with a design decision. Name each missing decision in the verdict.
- Fail the round when the plan makes a decision that the task file does not state. It belongs under the open questions.
- Check that the plan names what changes, the job of each change, the checks, the order of commits, the risks and the open questions.
- Check that the workspace holds `plan.md`. Do not fail the round for a clone of the repo.
